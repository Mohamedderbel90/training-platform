# Business Decisions Register

- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Purpose:** single place where ambiguities, conflicts and gaps found across
  the BRD, SRS, ERD, API Contract, UI Flow, `PROJECT_SPEC` and the ADRs are
  settled. The `.docx` sources are not edited (see `docs/README.md`); where
  their text conflicts with a decision below, **this register wins** for
  anything not explicitly BRD-approved, and the BRD wins otherwise.
- **How decisions were made:** each one is derived from approved BRD text
  (FR / BR / D ids) and the implemented behaviour. Nothing here adds a feature
  outside the BRD scope. Status values:
  - **Adopted** - derived from approved text; no further approval needed.
  - **Adopted - owner instruction** - decided by the product owner earlier.
  - **Adopted - ratified 2026-10-03 (DEC-46)** - the most coherent reading,
    ratified when the project owner signed off the BRD.
  - **Follow-up** - the rule is decided, the implementation does not yet match.

Open items that cannot be derived from the existing material are in section 4.

## 1. Decisions

### 1.1 Lifecycle and workflow

| ID | Decision | Basis | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-01 | **Training-day state is changed manually** by the General Supervisor in Odoo (open, close, reopen, postpone, cancel). No time-based automatic open/close. The survey window (`survey_open_at` / `survey_close_at`) is independent: submitting needs the day `open` **and** the current time inside the window. SRS 5.2 "or when the open time arrives" means the window opening, not a state change. | BRD FR-013, FR-018, D-16, section 7 (GS manages days); ADR-004 | Adopted |
| DEC-02 | **Allowed day transitions:** planned to open/postponed/cancelled; open to closed/postponed/cancelled; postponed to open/cancelled (a new date is set first); closed to open (Reopen, audited); cancelled is terminal. While a day is not `open`, no survey or attendance is accepted. | SRS 5.2; ADR-004 | Adopted |
| DEC-03 | **Missed submission.** A trainee cannot submit after the window or when the day is not open. The sanctioned remedy is the General Supervisor reopening the day or changing the window (both GS-controlled, reopen audited). The GS does **not** submit on a trainee's behalf: a response is bound to its submitter (FR-026). BRD section 6 "check/view" for the GS on submit rows means **view**. | BRD FR-018, FR-026, BR-004, section 6 | Adopted - ratified 2026-10-03 (DEC-46) |
| DEC-04 | **Attendance is editable only while the day is `open`**; locked once closed, postponed or cancelled. Late correction = GS reopens the day (audited), then the supervisor edits. Viewing is never blocked. | Owner instruction 2026-10-01 (OPD-12); SRS 5.2 reopen; BRD section 7 | Adopted - owner instruction |
| DEC-05 | **Explicit attendance per trainee.** Every active enrollee needs a saved status (present / absent / late). Nothing is pre-selected in the UI; the supervisor chooses a status per trainee (or uses the explicit "Mark remaining as Present" action) and only chosen rows are saved. Trainees with no saved status are "not recorded" and are excluded from rates; the day shows attendance as incomplete. | BRD FR-031, BR-006; ADR-011 (attendance complete) | Adopted - implemented 2026-10-03: nothing is pre-selected, only chosen rows are saved, an explicit "Mark remaining as Present" action exists, Save is disabled while nothing is chosen ; **2026-10-04:** the Odoo attendance record has no default status either |
| DEC-06 | **"Late"** = the supervisor marks arrival after the scheduled start. The business defines **no grace period or minute threshold**; the supervisor's judgement applies. Minutes/arrival time are optional (Should). "Late" counts as attended and is also in the late rate. | BRD FR-031, FR-032; SRS 6.7; R7 | Adopted |
| DEC-07 | **Enrollment lifecycle.** Enrollment is per program. `active` enrollees appear in attendance lists, response-rate targets and reminders. `inactive` / `completed` enrollees are excluded from **new** days; their history (attendance, responses) is kept and never hard-deleted. | BRD FR-014, BR-010; ERD section 14; ADR-011 | Adopted - ratified 2026-10-03 (DEC-46) ; **access enforced 2026-10-04**: only an active enrollment gives access to a day's survey (`_check_role_ownership`); inactive/completed enrollees keep only the read-back of what they already submitted |
| DEC-08 | **Assignments.** Trainer and supervisor are assigned per day by the GS in Odoo (one supervisor per day in v1, one or more trainers). Replacing an assignee later never deletes a report already saved by the previous trainer; each trainer has an independent report. | BRD FR-012, FR-017, D-05; SRS AC-SRS-006 | Adopted |
| DEC-09 | **Roles per account.** BRD section 18 ("each user has an independent account; permissions follow the role") is read as **one operational role per account**. A person needing two roles uses two accounts. | BRD section 18 | Adopted - ratified 2026-10-03 (DEC-46) ; **enforced 2026-10-04**: a constraint on `res.users` allows only one of trainee, supervisor, trainer per account |
